What Schemami does—and does not do

Schemami provides the recipe model, strict validation, exact calculations, stable diagnostics, and SDKs. It does not host an AI model, upload your source, store recipes, or manage provider credentials. The AI proposes; deterministic tooling decides whether the candidate is trustworthy enough to compute with.
