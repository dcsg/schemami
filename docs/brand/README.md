# Schemami brand system

Identity A / **Bounded Savor** is the founder-approved production direction as
of 10 August 2026.

## Canonical documents

| Document | Authority |
|---|---|
| [Brand foundation](schemami-brand-foundation.md) | Purpose, audience, positioning, name and claims |
| [Identity standard](schemami-identity-standard.md) | Logo, color, typography and visual rules |
| [Voice playbook](schemami-voice-playbook.md) | Messaging, tone, terminology and operational copy |
| [Design system](../../design-system/README.md) | Tokens, fonts, assets, component foundations and build workflow |
| [Visual standard](../../design-system/specimen.html) | Rendered production reference using the real assets and tokens |

If these sources conflict, the narrower canonical document wins: implementation
comes from `design-system/`, identity rules come from the identity standard,
voice rules come from the voice playbook, and strategy comes from the foundation.

The generated HTML companions use the approved production identity as fixed
reading documents. Theme controls belong to the design-system specimen and
product surfaces. Historical visual labs retain their original comparison
surfaces so the decision evidence is not recolored by the winning direction.

## Approved identity

- **Mark:** open Ink brackets containing a Marine connected `S` and three
  Brass/Clay/Brass nodes.
- **Wordmark/display:** Fraunces Variable.
- **Interface and prose:** IBM Plex Sans.
- **Code and identifiers:** IBM Plex Mono.
- **Identity colors:** Page `#FBF7EF`, Ink `#231A20`, Marine `#235D73`, Clay
  `#A94436`, Brass `#D09A22`.
- **Tagline:** “Recipes, structured to taste.”
- **Descriptor:** “The flexible recipe protocol for software.”

## Decision record

The founder chose A after reviewing the controlled
[identity decision lab](evaluation/schemami-identity-decision-lab.html). The
original identity and palette also led the ProductResearcher specialist reviews.
Ferment Lab remained close in numerical contrast and expert scoring, but its
green surfaces were less comfortable for repeated reading. Recursive remained a
credible technical type challenger, but did not outweigh the ownership and
culinary warmth of Fraunces plus Plex.

The approval authorizes consolidation of identity, tokens, design-system
foundations and voice rules. It does not assert trademark clearance, external
developer validation, universal script coverage or a completed technical
namespace migration.
