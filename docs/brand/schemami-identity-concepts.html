Selected synthesis

Develop Bounded Savor.

Keep option 2’s bracketed symbol and all three connection nodes, option 1’s serif wordmark and option 3’s palette. Refine spacing and optical weight after testing the mark at favicon and package-registry sizes.

Do not decide from novelty

Test recognition.

Show each symbol without its name, then with the wordmark. Ask what it suggests, which one feels trustworthy in SDK documentation, and which one remains recognizable a day later.
